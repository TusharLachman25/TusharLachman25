## About Me

I spent three years as a medical assistant in a Jakarta family practice, digitising
patient records by hand. Then I built the software that replaced that job — and the
practice now licenses it on a paid monthly subscription. That is the short version of
how I got here: I build things people keep using, and I stay to support them.

Almost everything below started as a problem someone I know actually had. A friend
group that argues about who owes what. A gym squad that never logs anything. My own
money split across two currencies, and my own week split across three calendars. The
interesting part is never the demo — it is the second week, when the data is messy,
the edge case shows up, and somebody still has to trust the number on the screen.

I work across full-stack, mobile, data and AI/ML, and I am open to **any** tech role —
software, data, ML, platform, QA or product.
